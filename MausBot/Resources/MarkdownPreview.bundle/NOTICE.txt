Markdown preview dependencies

The preview uses swift-cmark-gfm 1.0.2 and its bundled cmark-gfm parser.
DOMPurify 3.4.17 is used to sanitize rendered HTML.

The following unmodified preview assets are from MiaoYan commit
278685238ebf4afcb12a9cc01eb39e62bf5a54fe:
  highlight.js 11.5.1, KaTeX, Mermaid, and KaTeX fonts.
MiaoYan's math protection algorithm is adapted in MarkdownMath.swift.

The corresponding license texts are in the licenses directory.
